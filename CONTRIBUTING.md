# Contributing

Thanks for the interest.

**Discussions are welcome.** Open an issue to ask questions, propose
ideas, point out bugs in the spec, or share use cases. Translations
of existing skills into Dillylang recipes are particularly
interesting as evidence of where the vocabulary holds and where it
breaks.

**Pull requests are not yet accepted.** The vocabulary is still in
flux and the maintainer wants tight control over its shape during
this phase. PRs will be re-enabled once interfaces stabilize.
